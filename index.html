130 Nm High-Torque Coupling & DFM Optimization

Engineered a high-torque mechanical coupling in SolidWorks to transmit dynamic power between a high-RPM BLDC motor and a planetary gearbox.

1. ASME Y14.5 GD&T & Fit Calculations: Applied Datum Reference Frames (DRFs) to critical bearing bores and shaft mating surfaces. Enforced ISO transitional fits (H7/h6) and total runout controls (<0.02 mm) to prevent vibration harmonics at high RPM.
2. DFM Vendor Collaboration (-9% Cost): Redesigned interior corner fillets to standard 3/8-inch tool diameters, eliminating a secondary 5-axis milling setup and reducing production run unit cost by 9%.
3. Rapid Prototyping & Assembly Check: Produced SLA (Formlabs) and FDM 3D prints to verify socket wrench clearances, fastener torque-tool accessibility, and bearing insertion before hard steel tooling release.
// DEEP DIVE: COMPUTATIONAL DYNAMICS

Custom 16-DOF BFS Plate Solver & PZT Sensing

Formulated and coded a coupled electromechanical FEM solver from first principles in MATLAB using Classical Laminated Plate Theory (CLPT).

1. Governing Equations & Matrix Formulation: Utilized 16-DOF Bogner-Fox-Schmit (BFS) C1-continuous elements. Derived mass [M], stiffness [K], and coupled piezoelectric matrices [Kuφ] directly from Hamilton's Principle.
2. Experimental Validation (<2.4% Error): Extracted natural frequencies via generalized eigenvalue solution ([K] - ω²[M] = 0). Benchmark correlation against Her & Chen (2022) achieved <2.4% error for the first 5 modes.

Guided Ultrasonic Wave Automation in Abaqus

Engineered an end-to-end Python automation framework for Abaqus/Explicit to simulate ultrasonic wave dispersion (>100 kHz).

1. CFL Stability & Mesh Discretization: Enforced strict Courant-Friedrichs-Lewy (CFL) stable time increments (Δt ≤ L_min / c_d) and fine spatial discretization (>10 elements per acoustic wavelength).
2. Headless Python Execution: Scripted automated .inp solver deck creation, batch cluster submission, and binary .odb data mining for automated FFT signal and dispersion curve generation.

Chassis Topology Optimization & Composites

Redesigned structural chassis brackets and landing gear mechanisms via density-based topology optimization (SIMP method).

1. SIMP Density Optimization (-32% Mass): Transitioned baseline 6061-T6 aluminum parts to 3K carbon fiber composites, reducing total structural weight from 7.4 kg to 5.0 kg while preserving stiffness.
2. Modal Separation & Harmonic Tuning: Tuned fundamental natural frequencies (>140 Hz) to maintain a >35% frequency margin above motor operational RPMs, preventing dynamic airframe resonance.
